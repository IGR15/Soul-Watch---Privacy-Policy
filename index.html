Shout Racers Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Previous version: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Shout Racers (the "Game") uses, why, and who it is shared with. The Game is made by Soul Watch ("we", "us"), based in Palestine. If you have questions, contact us at soulwatch.in@gmail.com.

In short: we do not run our own servers, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking. The Game uses Steam (Valve) to sign you in and for multiplayer lobbies, connections, invites and the network that carries voice chat. Matches are hosted on a player's computer. Voice commands are recognised on your own computer.

2. Voice commands

The Game lets you use items by speaking. Speech recognition for voice commands runs entirely on your own computer. The microphone audio used for voice commands is processed in memory and then discarded: it is not saved, and it is not sent to us or to any speech service.

3. Information stored on your computer

Your settings, key bindings and racer choices are saved locally on your computer and are not sent to us. If the Game writes technical log or crash files, these also stay on your computer; they only reach us if you choose to send them to us, for example when reporting a bug.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry or tracking services.
  • We do not record or store voice chat or voice commands.

5. Third-party services

Steam handles information under Valve's own privacy policy: Steam Privacy Policy. The Game does not use any other online service.

Matches are hosted by players. When you join a match, the host's computer and the other players in it receive the gameplay information and voice described above.

6. Legal basis (EU / UK players)

We process the information above because it is necessary to provide the Game you have chosen to play, including its online multiplayer (performance of a contract), and, for voice chat, because you choose to turn it on and use your microphone. You can turn voice chat off, change push-to-talk, mute your microphone, or turn on Use Relay at any time in Settings.

7. How long information is kept

We do not keep any of the information above on our own systems. Information you send us directly, such as a bug report or an email, is kept only as long as needed to deal with it. Valve keeps information according to its own policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information about you is held, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because we do not store your data ourselves, most requests are handled by Valve through the link above. You can still contact us at soulwatch.in@gmail.com and we will help. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent to online services where you live, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe a child has used the Game's online features, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
